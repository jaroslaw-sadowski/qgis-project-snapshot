about=EN: Take your QGIS project offline with vectors, lossless maps, styles and a report. It downloads in parallel, adapts to your computer and servers, and resumes interrupted work. You'll need QGIS 3.40+/4.x, Python >=3.10 and GDAL >=3.7 supplied by QGIS, access to your sources and disk space. No extra Python packages. Install the release ZIP through the QGIS Plugin Manager, then restart QGIS.
    It runs locally, without analytics or sending projects/diagnostics to the author. Requests send the required area, layer settings and authentication to your chosen services. Please respect their licenses, attribution and download/reuse limits; the author isn't liable for users' violations to the extent permitted by law. GPL-2.0-only. Built with help from AI/vibe coding and tested (Bandit, secret scanning, Qt6 checks, Ruff/Flake8, proxy/error handling, integrity and crash recovery).
